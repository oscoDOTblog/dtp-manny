## Shared prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square Mandarin flashcard illustration
> (`<card id>`, `<meaning>`). Subject: `<subject below>`. Style: authentic retro
> 16-bit console pixel art, crisp hard-edged square pixels, limited cohesive
> colors, simple two-step shading, no anti-aliasing. Backdrop: plain warm ivory.
> Composition: centered, immediately recognizable, generous padding, readable at
> 200dp. Constraints: square; exact requested item count where applicable; no
> letters, Hanzi, pinyin, labels, captions, written numbers, border, watermark,
> UI, or unrelated objects.
